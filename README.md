## About me👩‍💻


Hi! I'm a Staff Data Analyst with 8+ years of experience driving product strategy and business growth through data.

My work sits at the intersection of Product Analytics, experimentation, and data strategy. I partner with Product, Engineering, and Design teams to build data-informed products, define meaningful metrics, and translate complex analyses into actionable business decisions.

Throughout my career, I've worked across financial services, marketplaces, consulting, and public-sector initiatives, leading analytics for high-impact products and designing experimentation frameworks that support better decision-making.

I'm particularly interested in AI-powered products, experimentation, and scalable analytics solutions that help teams move faster with confidence.

Beyond my day-to-day work, I co-founded Mulheres em Dados, one of Brazil's largest women-in-data communities, connecting thousands of data professionals and contributing to the growth of Brazil's data ecosystem.

Based in Brasília, Brazil · Open to connecting with data professionals worldwide.
